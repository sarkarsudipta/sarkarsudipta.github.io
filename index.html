About

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Computational and Data Sciences at IISER Kolkata, where I am advised by Prof. Dwaipayan Roy. My research interests broadly encompass learning and understanding across vision, language, and information retrieval, with a particular focus on developing efficient and reliable methods for multimodal retrieval and reasoning, alongside research on resource-efficient visual and video understanding, representation learning, and adaptation.

Projects

Resource-Efficient Learning for Video Scene Understanding (RLV)
JRF Project · IIT Kharagpur · 2025 – 2026
Developing lightweight spatiotemporal models for efficient video scene parsing and action recognition. Leveraging state-of-the-art architectures including DeiT, Reversible MViT, ATLAS, Swin Transformer, Hiera, and ViT baselines. Key contributions include integration of FlashAttention-2 for faster and memory-efficient training, knowledge distillation from large pretrained models, multimodal fusion (RGB + optical flow), and SIFAR-based video-to-image reformulation.
Tools: PyTorch, FlashAttention-2, einops, ViT, MViT, RevMViT, Swin, Hiera, DeiT, ATLAS, DINOv3, SIFAR
Supervisor: Prof. Abir Das, IIT Kharagpur
Details coming soon
GRTS: A Gated Residual Teacher–Student Framework
Independent Research · 2026
Proposed a feature-level teacher–student refinement framework where a frozen self-supervised DINOv2 teacher provides stable semantic priors and a trainable ResNet-50 student learns dataset-specific residuals via a learnable gated fusion mechanism. Achieved 97.8% Top-1 accuracy on UC Merced and 96.8% on AID aerial image benchmarks.
Tools: PyTorch, DINOv2, ResNet-50, Gated Residual Fusion
GitHub
Super Image for Efficient Large-Scale Video Action Recognition
M.Sc. Thesis · 2025 · Advisor: Prof. Abir Das
Innovated a frame-aggregation pipeline using the SIFAR framework to recast video action recognition as image classification. Frames are rearranged via einops into super-images (e.g., 3×3 grid of 224×224 frames → 672×672 or 4×4 grid → 896×896) and processed with ImageNet-21K pretrained Hiera-ViT and ViT models. Achieved ~79% accuracy on Kinetics-400 and ~64% on Something-Something V2.
Tools: PyTorch, Hiera Vision Transformer, ViT, SIFAR, einops
GitHub
Image Steganography and Steganalysis
RKMRC · 2024
Developed a deep learning framework for stego/non-stego image classification. Extracted texture features using Gray Level Co-occurrence Matrix (GLCM), followed by pretrained ResNet50 for feature encoding, LSTM for sequence modeling, and a classification head.
Tools: PyTorch, ResNet50, LSTM, GLCM
Supervisors: Dr. Siddhartha Banerjee & Bibek Ranjan Ghosh

Facial Expression Recognition
B.Sc. Final Year Project · 2023
Engineered a compact pretrained VGG16-based model for real-time detection of 7 facial emotions (anger, disgust, fear, happiness, sadness, surprise, neutral) on the FER-2013 dataset. Emphasized quantization and low-resource optimization for potential mobile HCI deployment.
